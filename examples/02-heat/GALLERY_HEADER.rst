Heat
====

The laser excitation of a sample structure is calculated from the absorption profile of the incident light, 
either by the Lambert-Beer law or by the multilayer formalism.
The resulting heat diffusion is solved for *N* coupled sub-systems, e.g. for electrons, phonons, and spins, 
with temperature-dependent material parameters.
This also includes the microscopic three-temperature model, in which a sub-system describes the magnetization.
