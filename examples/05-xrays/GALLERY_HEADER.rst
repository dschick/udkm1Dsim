X-rays
======

The static and transient X-ray response of a sample structure is calculated in the kinematical or dynamical scattering formalism.
Transient strain, temperature, and magnetization maps of the other simulations alter the diffraction or reflectivity, e.g. by Bragg peak shifts, the Debye-Waller effect, and resonant magnetic scattering in the XUV and soft X-ray range.
The simulations can also be fitted to experimental data.
