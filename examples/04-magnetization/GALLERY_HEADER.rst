Magnetization
=============

The magnetization dynamics of the layers and their sublattices are calculated from the transient temperature 
and strain maps of the other simulations.
The Landau-Lifshitz-Gilbert equation (LLG) describes the precession and relaxation of the magnetization with a prescribed amplitude, 
and its inertial extension (iLLG) also the nutation.
The Landau-Lifshitz-Bloch equation (LLB) additionally describes the dynamics of the amplitude, e.g. close to the Curie temperature, 
and with an exchange relaxation between sublattices the helicity-independent all-optical switching of ferrimagnets.
